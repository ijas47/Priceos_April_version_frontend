## 2. Product / How It Works

**H1:** From signal to approved price change — in one daily review.

**Intro:**
Most pricing tools stop at a recommendation and leave the rest to you. PriceOS runs the whole loop:
gather the signals, do the analysis, propose the change, check it against your rules, push it, and
verify it landed. Here's the loop, end to end.

---

**Step 1 — It reads your portfolio every morning.**
The Data Aggregator syncs your listings, calendar, and reservations from your PMS. PriceOS always
works from today's truth, not last week's export.

**Step 2 — It gathers the signals you can't track by hand.**
Local events with real demand pull. Competitor rates on comparable units. Forward demand by date.
Open gaps too short to book at standard minimum-stay.

**Step 3 — It proposes specific, priced actions.**
Not "consider raising prices." A number, a date range, a property, and the reason — "Bay View 1BR,
lower to AED 410 for the orphan Tuesday night, gap-fill to avoid a dead date."

**Step 4 — It checks its own work.**
The Adjustment Reviewer runs every proposal against your floors, ceilings, max daily move, and risk
thresholds. Anything outside the lines gets held or flagged, not pushed.

**Step 5 — You approve.**
Routine, low-risk changes can clear automatically if you allow it. The bold calls wait for you.
Either way, you see the reasoning.

**Step 6 — It executes and confirms.**
Channel Sync writes approved changes to your PMS and verifies the new price is actually live — so a
silent API failure never costs you a weekend at the wrong rate.

## 3. Features — Dynamic Pricing

**H1:** Pricing that adjusts for the reasons you'd adjust it for — if you had the time.

**Intro:**
PriceOS doesn't just chase a demand curve. It prices the way a sharp revenue manager would, with
rules you can read and override.

**Capability blocks:**

- **Event-aware pricing.** When a market event will move demand on specific dates, PriceOS prices
  for it — ahead of the booking wave, not after.
- **Competitor-aware rates.** It positions each unit against comparable listings, so you're not the
  cheapest room in a sold-out market or the most expensive in a soft one.
- **Gap optimization.** Orphan nights and unbookable two-night holes get their own treatment —
  targeted discounts and minimum-stay overrides that turn dead dates into revenue.
- **Seasonal rules, set for you.** PriceOS reads the seasonal shape of your market and generates
  peak/shoulder/low-season rules automatically. Edit any of them.
- **Day-of-week, last-minute, and far-out logic.** Weekend uplift, last-minute discounts, far-out
  markups — configured per property, applied every night.
- **Floors and ceilings you own.** Set the range. PriceOS optimizes inside it and never steps
  outside.

**Kicker:** Set your strategy once — Conservative, Balanced, or Aggressive — and PriceOS configures
every property and rule to match. Re-baseline your whole portfolio in one click.

## 5. Features — Guest Inbox & Reservations

**H1:** Reply to every guest in minutes — in your voice, with the booking already loaded.

**Intro:**
Guest messaging eats your evenings and your team's. PriceOS drafts the reply so you're editing, not

PriceOS will never message a guest you didn't sign off on.

## 11. FAQ

**Will it change my prices without asking?**
No. PriceOS proposes; you approve. You can let routine low-risk changes auto-clear, but you set
that threshold — and you can run it fully read-only.

**How is this different from PriceLabs or Beyond?**
Those are tools you operate. PriceOS is a revenue manager that operates *for* you — it does the
analysis, makes specific recommendations with reasoning, executes the approved ones, and verifies
they landed.

**What PMS do you support?**
Hostaway today, with more on the roadmap. PriceOS reads your full portfolio and writes only
approved changes back.

**Can I try it without risking my live prices?**
Yes. Run it in read-only/observe mode. It analyzes your real portfolio and shows what it would do,
while writing nothing back.

**Does it message my guests automatically?**
No. It drafts replies with full context; you review and send. Outbound sending is off by default.

**How long to set up?**
