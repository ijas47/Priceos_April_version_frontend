## Slide 2 — The Problem

**Property managers leave money on the table every single night.**

A manager running 15–50 units faces **hundreds of pricing micro-decisions a day**:
- Which units need a price change today?
- Is this empty night an orphan gap or a demand signal?
- Did a competitor drop rates from weakness, or a pricing error?
- Is the F1 weekend / festival already priced in, or not yet?

Today they juggle **PMS dashboards + spreadsheets + WhatsApp + gut feel**.
The result: **stale pricing, missed gaps, slow event response — revenue leakage**.

_Land the emotional truth: it's not lack of data, it's decision fatigue at scale._

## Slide 4 — The Solution

**PriceOS is a centralized decision board: See → Decide → Act → Verify.**

1. **Aggregate** — calendars, bookings, market rates, local events in one view.
2. **Propose** — a recommended price for every night, with a written reason and a risk badge.
3. **Approve** — operator approves per-night or in bulk. Nothing acts silently.
4. **Execute & verify** — approved changes sync to the PMS and are confirmed.

**You talk to Aria, your AI revenue manager. It does the analysis; you keep control.**

_This is the "aha." Emphasize the operator stays in the driver's seat._
